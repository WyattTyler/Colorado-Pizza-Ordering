Welcome to Colorado Pizza

At Colorado Pizza, we believe great food starts with great ingredients. Our dough is made fresh every morning, hand‑tossed by our chefs who have perfected the craft over years of experience. Every pizza is layered with rich, slow‑simmered tomato sauce, premium cheeses, and toppings sourced from trusted local farms. Whether you're grabbing a quick slice or sitting down for a family dinner, we’re committed to delivering flavor, freshness, and a warm Colorado welcome every time you visit.

Our restaurant blends rustic charm with modern comfort — cozy booths, warm lighting, and the aroma of wood‑fired pizza filling the air. We’re proud to be a community favorite, serving Colorado and beyond with passion, quality, and a whole lot of cheese.
